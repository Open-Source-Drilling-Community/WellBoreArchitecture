### Confirmed domain decisions — preserve these

- CasingSection.TopDepth and TopCementDepth are **along-hole coordinates**, not vertical depths.
- Along-hole depth is a curvilinear abscissa, independent of whether planned, computed or measured. Prefer this terminology over MeasuredDepth.
- Canonical along-hole zero is the path's **intersection or defined extension to the WGS84 ellipsoid**. It is not obtained merely by applying a vertical elevation offset.
- WellHead.Depth, CasingHangerDepth, TubingHangerDepth and environmental-fluid layer Depth are **vertical** coordinates. Fluid Depth is the **top boundary** of its layer.
- SideConnector.Position is **local**, zero at the host top and increasing downward along the host. It is not a global WGS84 depth.
- Physical extents use LengthStandard; borehole/pipe diameters use DiameterPipeDrilling. Extents are distinct from coordinates.
- TensileStrength is stress (DrillStemMaterialStrengthDrilling, Pa). TensileCapacity is a distinct ForceDrilling concept; no provider field or implicit stress-to-force calculation was added.
- TorsionalStrength binds to TorsionalCapacity/TorqueDrilling. LinearWeight is mass per length. Burst/collapse capacities are pressure differences, not vacuum-referenced absolute pressure.
- Uncertainty does not inherit the mean's coordinate/pressure origin. Null deviation means unspecified, not zero. Bounds are domain limits, not confidence limits or Gaussian truncation instructions.
- Canonical references apply to **storage and APIs**. User-selected MSL, ground/mud-line and other supported presentation references must remain available. Do not remove editor reference selectors or reinterpret persisted data.
- More generally, approved OSDC conventions include true-north azimuths, WGS84-normal internal inclination, WGS84 Riemannian coordinates and vacuum-referenced absolute pressure, where applicable.
